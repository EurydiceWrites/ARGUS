# Reading List — UAP Classification Systems (Primary Sources)

**Purpose:** Direct primary sources for the five UAP classification systems referenced in the H-005 notebook. Use these to form your own view; the H-005 notebook itself is a rough starting map that may contain errors and oversimplifications.

**Created:** 2026-05-12

**Honesty caveat:** I'm more confident about some entries than others. Where I am uncertain, I say so. Verify any citation before relying on it in a final document.

---

## 1. J. Allen Hynek

- Hynek, J. Allen. 1972. *The UFO Experience: A Scientific Inquiry.* Chicago: Henry Regnery.
  - The book where he introduces the NL / DD / RV + CE-1 / 2 / 3 classification. Roughly 280 pages. Still in print (reprinted by various publishers; used copies are inexpensive).

- Hynek, J. Allen. 1977. *The Hynek UFO Report.* New York: Dell.
  - Applies the classification to Project Blue Book cases. Useful supplement, not foundational.

---

## 2. Jacques Vallée

His classification framework develops across multiple books rather than appearing in a single canonical text. Books to consider:

- Vallée, Jacques. 1969. *Passport to Magonia: From Folklore to Flying Saucers.* Chicago: Henry Regnery.

- Vallée, Jacques. 1988. *Dimensions: A Casebook of Alien Contact.* Chicago: Contemporary Books.

- Vallée, Jacques. 1990. *Confrontations: A Scientist's Search for Alien Contact.* New York: Ballantine.

He has also published in the *Journal of Scientific Exploration* (JSE) — their archive is searchable at https://www.scientificexploration.org/journal-library.

**Honesty note:** I am NOT certain which single Vallée publication is the original venue of the AN / FB / MA / CE × 1–5 typology specifically. *Confrontations* (1990) is where I expected to find it, but verify directly.

## 3. GEIPAN (institutional — no single canonical book)

- GEIPAN. "Classification." https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/58787

- GEIPAN. "Methodology." https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/58788

- GEIPAN. "What is the methodology used?" https://www.cnes-geipan.fr/en/node/416

**Adjacent (not GEIPAN itself):** The COMETA Report (1999) — a French unofficial report by retired military and aerospace officials, in the same investigative tradition. I am not certain enough of its exact French title to put a full citation here; if you go looking, search "COMETA Report 1999" rather than relying on my memory.

## 4. AARO

Read with the methodological skepticism you've already named. AARO is a primary-source provider of *data*, not an authoritative classifier.

- AARO official site: https://www.aaro.mil/

- AARO UAP Records: https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Records/

- AARO. 2024. *Report on the Historical Record of U.S. Government Involvement with Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP), Volume I.* Released March 2024. Available via the AARO and DoD websites.

- AARO Mission Brief 2025: https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Mission_Brief_2025.pdf

- ODNI UAP annual reports — search https://www.dni.gov/ for "UAP annual report." Releases began in 2021.

---

## 5. Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies (SCU)

- SCU official site: https://www.explorescu.org/

- About SCU: https://www.explorescu.org/about-us

- SCU's Zenodo community (full publications, peer-reviewed): https://zenodo.org/communities/explorescu/records

- SCU. 2023. *UAP Activity Pattern Study 1945–1975: Military and Public Activities.* Zenodo. https://zenodo.org/records/8213330

SCU does not have a single canonical classification document — their nine activity types are derived across publications. Sample 2-3 recent papers rather than expecting a single foundational text.
